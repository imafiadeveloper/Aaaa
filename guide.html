Beginner's Guide

1. How to Register

  1. Fill your phone number
  2. Set your own password (6 letters)
  3. Fill your Recommendation code (Optional)
  4. Click Register

2. How to Deposit

  1. Go to Wallet > Deposit
  2. Enter the amount
  3. Scan the QR code and pay
  4. Enter the 12-digit UTR number and submit
